# Trademark policy

The Nightjar name and logo identify the official project. This policy says what
you may do without asking. It does not limit the rights the GPL-3.0-only licence
grants you to use, modify or redistribute the software.

## Use that is fine

- Refer to Nightjar accurately in text, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Say that your product or service is compatible with Nightjar, or works with
  it, when the statement is true.
- Redistribute an unmodified official release, with its name and notices intact.

## Use that needs permission

- Do not use the Nightjar name or logo as the name or branding of a fork,
  modified build, product, service or domain.
- Do not imply that an unofficial build, fork or service is official, endorsed
  or supported by the project.
- Do not use the logo in a way that suggests endorsement.

When in doubt, ask a maintainer before you use the name or logo.
