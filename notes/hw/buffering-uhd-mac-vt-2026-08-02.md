# UHD Mac Videotoolbox buffering (2026-08-02)

**Claim class:** dogfood observation, not measured. Not a colour/tonemap claim.

During product HLS web-player inspection of UHD MakeMKV titles (P7 MEL, P7 FEL,
P8.1) on the founder Mac with `h264_videotoolbox`, Garrett reported buffering
about every 1–2 seconds. HDR10 / P8.4 synthetic 720p titles were not called out
the same way.

Held here so UI work on playback chrome / buffering states has a pointer.
Not discarded.
